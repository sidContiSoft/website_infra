Company profile

Engineering and infrastructure,
under one roof.

Supreme Electromech Power & Infra works where precision engineering meets large-scale infrastructure. We design, fabricate and deliver pre-engineered steel buildings, specialised metal roofing and insulated envelopes, utility systems and fire protection — as a single accountable scope.

Our engineering office models every structure in MBS, STAAD.Pro and TEKLA Structures, detailing frames and connections against AISC, MBMA, AWS, ASCE and Indian Standards. Our works at Faridabad and New Delhi support fabrication, tooling and assembly for projects across India.

A precision toolroom discipline sits beneath all of it: components are measured, inspected and documented before they leave the works. That is why our buildings fit together on site — and why clients return with their next facility.
