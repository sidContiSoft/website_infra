Standing seam & high rib

Concealed-fix roofing

Standing seam profiles eliminate through-fasteners on the roof, allowing thermal movement while maintaining a weather-tight joint. High-rib sheets provide deeper sections for long spans and strong architectural lines.

  • Base steel 0.47 – 0.60 mm, colour-coated Galvalume
  • Long-length sheets with controlled expansion
  • Coordinated ridge, eave, valley and penetration flashings

MS deck sheets

Composite floor decks

Deck sheets act as permanent formwork and positive reinforcement for mezzanine slabs, reducing concrete and reinforcement requirements while speeding up the floor cycle.

  • Thickness 0.90 – 1.20 mm galvanised steel
  • Profile depth matched to the floor span and loading
